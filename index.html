About Me

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Commerce (Finance). After discovering my strong interest in cybersecurity, I made a strategic career transition into this rapidly evolving domain. Understanding that a solid foundation in networking is essential for cybersecurity, I pursued and completed a Diploma in Computer Hardware and Network Engineering (DHNE).

To further strengthen my technical expertise, I completed the Certified IT Infrastructure & Cyber Security Analyst (CICSA) program from RedTeam Hacker Academy. I also earned the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) certification from EC-Council, which enhanced my skills in ethical hacking, threat analysis, and security assessment.

Currently, I am continuing to advance my knowledge by pursuing a Master of Computer Applications (MCA) along with CCNA and CCNP certifications, with a strong focus on networking, security architecture, and advanced troubleshooting.

I am deeply committed to continuous learning and professional growth in cybersecurity. I actively engage in self-driven learning through hands-on practice, industry research, and ongoing upskilling to stay aligned with emerging threats and evolving technologies. I aspire to contribute effectively to securing digital assets, strengthening organizational security postures, and delivering impactful cybersecurity solutions.
