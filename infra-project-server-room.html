Physical Rack Mounting & Dual PDU Power Failover

Eliminating single points of failure at the power distribution and chassis level.

Following successful bench staging, all network hardware was permanently rack-mounted into the 42U server cabinet. The chassis layout was planned for optimal thermal airflow (front-to-back cold-aisle / hot-aisle) and balanced weight distribution:

Chassis Allocation
  • • Dual FortiGate 200G (HA Pair)
  • • Dual Arista 720DP Core Switches
  • • 4× Arista Access Switches
  • • 2× Dedicated Arista AP Switches
Dual PDU Power Architecture
  • • PSU-1 wired to PDU Feed A
  • • PSU-2 wired to PDU Feed B
  • • Dual C13/C14 power cords per box
  • • Independent electrical branch circuits
Live UPS Failover Test
  • • Simulated grid power failure
  • • UPS online battery switchover: 0ms
  • • Zero chassis reboot or flap
  • • Continuous load runtime verified

Both switches and firewalls are equipped with dual internal hot-swappable power supplies. Pulling either power feed during active packet forwarding resulted in zero packet drop and seamless power transfer.

Structured Cabling Dressing, Rack Earthing & Alphanumeric Labeling

High-density patch management adhering to enterprise data center standards.

Clean cable management directly protects against accidental unplugging, signal crosstalk, and thermal air dams:

Cable Comb Routing & Velcro Ties

Yellow Cat6 patch cables were bundled using specialized cable combs to keep individual runs parallel and strain-free. Reusable hook-and-loop velcro straps were used exclusively instead of plastic zip-ties to prevent crushing internal copper twists.

Alphanumeric Aligned Port Labeling

Every single patch run received printed heat-shrink and wrap-around labels matching destination patch panels: D01-D120 for client workstations, RD01-RD48 for redundancy links, and WF01-WF12 for wireless access points.

Rack Earthing & Grounding Verification
The 42U steel cabinet was bonded to the main data center copper ground busbar using low-impedance 6 AWG green/yellow grounding conductors. Resistance to ground was tested to verify less than 1Ω of ground loop impedance, protecting sensitive ASIC silicon from static and surge damage.

Facility Defense: eSSL Biometric Security & Dual-AC Climate Stability

Protecting physical assets with biometric perimeter controls and thermal redundancy.

eSSL Biometric / RFID Access Control

Installed and configured an eSSL smart access control system on the server room security door with electromagnetic locks. Only pre-authorized engineering personnel can enter via verified biometric fingerprint or RFID smart badge.

• Real-time timestamped ingress/egress logging
• Door held-open alarm & forced-entry prevention

Dual Precision AC Climate Redundancy

Server room temperature is managed by dual redundant air conditioning units configured in an active-cycling configuration. Tested auto-switching to verify that if one AC unit fails or trips, the secondary unit immediately compensates.

• Ambient room temperature maintained at 20°C ± 2°C
• Relative humidity stabilized between 45% – 55%

Arista AP-C460 Wi-Fi 7 & Dual-PoE Power Failure Resilience

Testing real-world failover when pulling PoE power lines under live client traffic.

The enterprise deployed next-generation Arista AP-C460 Wi-Fi 7 Access Points. To guarantee zero wireless dropouts for executive and campus users, APs were dual-homed across two separate AP switches with dual 802.3bt/at Power over Ethernet (PoE) drops:

LIVE PoE LINK-PULL EXPERIMENTAL VALIDATION:
Test Step 1: Connected active clients streaming high-bandwidth data through AP-C460.
Test Step 2: Physically unplugged PoE line 1 from AP Switch 1.
Test Step 3: Evaluated instantaneous client throughput and ICMP ping status.
Observed Result 1: AP-C460 instantaneously drew power from PoE line 2 with zero reboot.
Observed Result 2: Continuous ping -t verified 0 dropped packets.
Observed Result 3: Zero Wi-Fi speed reduction; no connected client disconnected.

Prior to AP deployment, every single ceiling drop and AP patch link was verified through Fluke DSX-5000 certification tests, confirming wiremap continuity, pin pairing, and maximum PoE loop resistance compliance.
