2

TIA Cat 6 Channel Testing Methodology

Full spectrum RF parameter verification across horizontal UTP cable runs.

Testing was configured for TIA Cat 6 Channel specifications following the T568B pinout standard. Unlike basic continuity testers that merely check voltage on pins, the Fluke DSX-5000 executes comprehensive multi-frequency sweeps up to 500 MHz, analyzing:

Wire Map & Length

Verifies 1-to-1 continuity on all 8 conductors and calculates physical link length (e.g. 47.8m on Link RD-10) using cable propagation velocity.

Insertion & Return Loss

Quantifies signal attenuation over distance (Insertion Loss: 18.0 dB) and reflections caused by impedance variations (Return Loss: 4.9 dB margin).

NEXT & PS-NEXT

Near-End Crosstalk (7.7 dB margin) and Power Sum NEXT (10.1 dB margin), confirming wire twists were maintained right up to the termination point.

3

Field Fault Detection, Re-Termination & 100% Retest PASS

Real-world troubleshooting: pinpointing defective connections and re-testing to perfection.

During testing of link D-164, the Versiv 2 analyzer immediately flagged a FAIL on the T568B Wire Map:

DIAGNOSTIC WIRE MAP RESULT: FAIL (Link D-164)
Pin 1 (White-Orange): OPEN at 30.8 meters (Discontinuity detected)
Pin 2 through 8: Continuous and correctly paired
Root Cause: Incomplete IDC punch-down contact inside the user-end keystone jack.

Using the exact 30.8-meter distance calculation provided by the Fluke TDR, the field team was dispatched directly to the exact workstation drop. The keystone was de-punched, re-stripped, and re-terminated using a calibrated impact punch-down tool with the jacket maintained within 0.5 inches of the terminal block.

POST-REMEDIATION RETEST: PASS* (Link D-119 / D-164)
All 8 conductors verified intact: Pair 1-2, Pair 3-6, Pair 4-5, Pair 7-8 verified in full compliance with T568B.
